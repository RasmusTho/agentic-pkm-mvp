# Contributing

Contributions and collaborations are welcome by prior discussion.

This repository is still unstable and is not currently released as open source.
Before opening a substantial pull request, issue, integration proposal, or
redistribution request, please contact Rasmus Thornberg so scope and licensing
expectations are clear.

## Contribution Terms

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you confirm that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution;
- the contribution is your original work or is compatible with this repository's
  licensing and third-party notice requirements;
- you grant Rasmus Thornberg a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
  royalty-free right to use, copy, modify, distribute, sublicense, and relicense
  the contribution as part of this project; and
- the contribution may be included in future versions released under different
  license terms, including more permissive or more restrictive terms.

If you cannot grant those rights, say so before submitting the contribution.

## Third-Party Content

Do not submit third-party code, datasets, model outputs, documents, images, or
other materials unless their source and license are clear and compatible with
the intended use. Include attribution and license notes with the contribution.

## No Implied Broad License

Discussion, issue comments, pull requests, or collaboration proposals do not
grant broader rights to use the repository than those stated in
[LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md), unless Rasmus Thornberg grants additional permission
in writing.
