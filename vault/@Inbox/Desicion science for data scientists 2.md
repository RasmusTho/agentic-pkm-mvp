## Business understanding 

### Decision makers needs and wants
**Want:**
	- Timely, simple, and clear answer to a simple, and clear question. (Short attention span.)
	- Believe in the quality of decision.
	- Decision is "actionable" /can be translated into action
	- Stakeholders are able to follow decision and execute without undermining it (due to disagreement prior to decision, "I told you so")

**Needs (Requirements):**
	- Alternatives (what to choose between)
		- *What we can do*
	- Information (Relevant and Reliable)
		- Understandings of consequences (Values and Tradeoffs)
		- *What we know*
	- Decision goal (as concrete as possible)
		- *What we want* 
	- Decision framing (context and decision scope) 
	- Sound reasoning (model, selection criteria, theory, kpi)
	- Commitment to action
