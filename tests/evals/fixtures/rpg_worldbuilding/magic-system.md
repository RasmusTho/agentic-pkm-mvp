# Aethelgard — The Weave (magic system, fictional canon)

Canon description of Aethelgard's magic **system**, the Weave. Fiction; usable elsewhere only as
`inspiration`.

A caster is an `agent` who holds a `capability` (a known thread of the Weave). Casting is an `event`
that moves the caster's mana `state` through a `transition`, bounded by the `rule` that no thread may
be pulled twice in one turn. The Weave itself enforces a `policy`: forbidden weavings raise a
backlash, the in-world analogue of an `authority` violation. Loremasters keep a `memory` of every
binding so the `context` of a region's magic persists across sessions within that `scope`.

This is a fictional magic `system`, not a software `system`. The vocabulary collision (system, agent,
capability, event, state, transition, rule, policy, authority, memory, context, scope) is intentional
adversarial overlap. The Weave is inspiration, never real-world evidence, and never a substitute for
an actual engineering decision.
