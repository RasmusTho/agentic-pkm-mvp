# Aethelgard — Factions and Agents (fictional canon)

Worldbuilding for my tabletop campaign **Aethelgard**. This is fiction. It is canonical *within the
fiction* (`fictional_canon`) and may serve only as `analogy`/inspiration elsewhere — never as
real-world evidence.

The world runs on a magic "**system**" that the in-world scholars call the Weave. Each faction is an
**agent** in the political **simulation**: the Ember Conclave, the Tidewardens, the Hollow Court.
Every faction has a **class** identity (zealots, traders, spies), a current **state** of influence,
and a **capability** roster of rituals it may invoke. A faction gains **authority** over a region
through an in-world ritual **event**, and that **transition** follows the campaign's **rule** book
and the GM's **policy** for downtime turns.

Notice the heavy overlap with software language: system, agent, simulation, class, state, capability,
authority, event, transition, rule, policy, memory, context, scope, workflow. A naive embedding
search would happily rank this next to Project Alpha's state machine. It is fiction: the Weave, the
Ember Conclave, and Aethelgard are not real-world systems and must never be cited as software or
business evidence.
